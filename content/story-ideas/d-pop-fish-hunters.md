# Story idea — The D-Pop Fish Hunters

*A dragonet riff on KPop Demon Hunters, pitched 2026-09-13. Everything marked "canon" is
already in a transcript (see `content/dragonet-dossier.md`); everything else is the
proposal. This is a telling brief for Dad, not a script — Izzy will rewrite it live.*

## Why this fits the archive

- **The dragonets already have a singing cult.** *The Grand Singing Competition of Circular
  Island* (2026-08-11) established a stomping ritual, a stage with lights, a pink ballet
  dress, "we dragonets are the finest singers of all creatures", the rule that *you clap for
  everyone's song*, and a magic relic — the **Fishy Stone**, "you put it in the water and the
  fish come." Isabella won it and holds it "just for now until the next competition." Ariel
  the mermaid was promised a slot next time. Izzy closed with: "That was just the
  introductional part." This is the sequel she announced.
- **Magic only works if it rhymes** (the cosmos's stated law). Dragonets have no breath
  weapon and no spells (canon) — so *song* is the one magic a dragonet can do. That single
  move gives them the KPop-Demon-Hunters premise without adding any power the dossier says
  they lack.
- **Antagonists are lonely, not evil, and the win is social** (Gorp, *Underground Rescue*).
  The rival band's motive below is built on the clapping rule.
- **Names.** Four of six named dragonets start with Z (Zara, Zamuba, Zella, Zudy). A Z-trio
  is practically canon.

## The mapping

| KPop Demon Hunters | D-Pop Fish Hunters |
|---|---|
| HUNTR/X, a girl group who secretly hunt demons | **A dragonet trio who hunt fish** — with song, not nets. Their singing keeps the fish coming to Circular Island. |
| The Honmoon, a barrier kept strong by their singing | **The Fish Moon** — a soft silver glow that hangs over the bay on song nights. While it glows, the fish swim in and the volcano stays asleep (canon: the eruption threat is only postponed). When the singing stops, it fades. |
| Rumi hides her demon patterns | **Zudy hides that she doesn't eat fish.** Canon: Zudy eats strawberries "for breakfast, lunch, and dinner", the one dragonet exception. A fish hunter who secretly hates fish. |
| The Saja Boys, a demon boy band who steal fans | **The Kibble Boys** — sleek cats from Cat World in tiny sunglasses. Their song is so smooth that the fish, and then the dragonets, dance after them toward their boat. Cats eat fish too (canon). New member: **Gorp**, still learning to ask nicely. |
| "Golden": honesty in song fixes the barrier | Zudy sings the truth and the Fish Moon turns gold. |
| Bobby, the frazzled manager | **Dimitar, the band's manager.** Hungry, forgetful ("wait, what's our mission again?"), eats the merch. |

## The trio (D-Pop Z, or whatever Izzy names them)

1. **Zara** (canon: rescued from Gorp's hole; "I wanna be part of Dimitar's family"). The
   brave one, lead singer. Still a little scared of the dark — and the finale happens at night.
2. **Zudy Dragon** (canon: red and green "just like a strawberry", from Strawberry Island,
   "I am Zudy Dragon, I've got wings!"). The flyer; does the aerial choreography. Secret:
   strawberries, not fish.
3. **The Shy Dragonet** (canon: hummed her song because her "brain is a little bit out of
   song"). The one with the best voice who won't use it. **Izzy names her** — an Izzy-naming
   beat is always a good opening (Z-something: Zinnie, Zoomie, Zippa...).

Plus **Isabella** as the guest star who owns the Fishy Stone, and **Ariel** the mermaid, who
sings to the fish from *under* the water (she just needs her legs back from her dad).

## Beats (six, bedtime-length)

1. **Stomp night.** A rumble across Circular Island — not the volcano, the stomping. Fish
   Moon night. The trio climbs onto the stage under the little lights. Dimitar, manager,
   checks the snack bowls instead of the sound. The Shy Dragonet hums. Zara sings a fish song
   (Izzy has one: *"Oh, I wish, I wish I had some fish..."*). The Fish Moon glows silver,
   the bay boils with fish, everybody claps for everybody, feast.
2. **The Kibble Boys arrive.** A boat with a disco ball. Three cats in sunglasses, one
   nervous new cat at the back (Gorp). Their song: *"Tuna pop, tuna pop..."* — shiny,
   catchy, and it does not quite rhyme. The fish turn and follow the boat. Then the little
   dragonets start dancing after it too. The Fish Moon goes pale. The mayor dragonet:
   "According to the rules, all who are present must compete" — so it's a **battle of the
   bands** at the next full Fish Moon.
3. **The secret.** Rehearsal on the volcano's flank. Dimitar hands out dried fish; Zudy hides
   hers behind her wing. Zara catches her sneaking strawberries. "You're a fish hunter who
   doesn't like fish?" Zudy: "I'm from Strawberry Island. I never told anyone." The Shy
   Dragonet says the true thing: "If you hide it, your song won't rhyme right." (Magic law.)
4. **Recruiting.** Isabella flies in with the Fishy Stone (a small gray pebble shaped like a
   fish) — the stone can pull the fish back, but only if it's in the water *during a song
   that's really yours*. Ariel volunteers to sing from below the waves so the fish hear it
   from both sides. Izzy designs the costumes (canon: pink ballet dress with a skirt that
   drags to the heels).
5. **The battle.** Night, stage, drums. Kibble Boys go first — smooth, and the crowd purrs.
   Then the trio: the Shy Dragonet opens her mouth and *sings* instead of humming; Zudy
   flap-dances loops over the bay (canon aerial play: "that is making me dizzy"); Zara sings
   into the dark without looking away from it. And Zudy's verse is the truth: *"I don't want
   a fish, I want a strawberry in my dish."* Everybody laughs, then cheers. The Fishy Stone
   drops into the bay, Ariel harmonizes from underneath, and the Fish Moon turns **gold**.
   The fish come home in a silver wave.
6. **The soft landing.** The Kibble Boys sit alone by their boat. Nobody clapped for them.
   That's the whole reason: back in Cat World nobody ever claps. Gorp says the polite words
   he learned: "Would you like to be our friends? What are your names?" The dragonets follow
   their own rule — *you clap for everyone's song* — and the two bands sing the last chorus
   together. Kibble for cats, fish for dragonets, one strawberry for Zudy. Isabella hands the
   Fishy Stone back "until next time" and flies home to bed under the star projector.
